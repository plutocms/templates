There is a café at the end of my street with a long wooden table by the window. Three mornings a week, I work there.

## Why it works

At home, there is always a reason to stop. At the café, there is only the table, the coffee, and the work.

## The rules

- One coffee per two hours of work
- Headphones on, phone off
- Leave before the lunch crowd

The owner knows my order now. Some days that is the only conversation I have before noon, and it is enough.
