We left on Friday after work and arrived at the cabin after dark.

## Saturday

A long trail to the lake, with a stop for lunch at the top. The water was too cold to swim, but we tried.

- 14 km in total
- 600 m up, and the same down
- One very tired dog

## Sunday

Rain all morning, so we read by the fire. In the afternoon the sky cleared, and we drove home slowly.
