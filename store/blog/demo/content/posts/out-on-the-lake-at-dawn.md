The lake is busy by nine. At six it belongs to the birds, and to anyone who can carry a canoe down the path in the dark.

## The first light

The water was so still that the trees on the far side looked twice as tall. I stopped paddling and let the canoe drift.

## What to bring

- A warm hat, even in summer
- Tea in a flask
- A dry bag for the phone you will not use

By the time the first motorboat came out, I was back on the shore, with cold hands and a very good mood.
