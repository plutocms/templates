This is my grandmother's method. It takes longer than the recipe on the box, and it is worth every minute.

## What you need

- 1 kg of small, ripe strawberries
- 600 g of sugar
- The juice of one lemon

## How

Cut the fruit, cover it with the sugar, and leave it overnight. The next day, cook it slowly for about forty minutes, until a drop on a cold plate holds its shape.

Fill clean, warm jars and close them at once. Keep one jar open for the toast.
