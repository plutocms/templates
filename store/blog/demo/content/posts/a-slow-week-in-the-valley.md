We rented a cabin by the river for a week and made one rule: no plans before breakfast.

## The days

Most mornings, we walked up the river until we were hungry. Most afternoons, we read on the rocks and swam when it got too hot.

## The nights

No signal, no screens, and more stars than I have seen in years. On the fourth night, we saw a fox at the door.

## Going home

The drive out of the valley took two hours. It felt like we had been away for a month.
