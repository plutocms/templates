We left on Friday after work and found the cabin after dark, with the help of a very old map.

## Saturday

A long trail to the ridge, with lunch at the top. The wind took my hat, and a sheep watched it go.

- 14 km in total
- 600 m up, and the same down
- Two very tired legs

## Sunday

Rain all morning, so we read by the stove. In the afternoon the sky cleared for an hour, and we drove home slowly, with the windows down.
