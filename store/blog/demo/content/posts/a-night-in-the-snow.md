I had camped many times, but never on snow. A friend with more experience said yes to a short trip, and we picked a valley two hours from the car.

## What went well

The tent stayed up in the wind, and the stove worked at minus eight. Dinner was hot noodles, and nothing ever tasted better.

## What I will change

- A second mat under the sleeping bag
- Boots inside the tent, not in the porch
- Fewer clothes in the pack, warmer ones

In the morning, the sun came over the ridge, and the whole valley turned gold for ten minutes.
