The first hour of the day sets the pace for the rest of it. Mine is simple, and it has not changed in two years.

## Coffee first

One cup, made by hand. It takes five minutes, and I do not look at a screen while the water heats.

## A short walk

Ten minutes outside, in any weather. The walk is not exercise. It is time to think before anyone asks me anything.

## One page of notes

Back at the table, I write one page. Plans, worries, a line I heard on the radio. Nobody reads it, and that is the point.

> A slow start is not a late start.

That is all. The rest of the day can be as loud as it wants.
