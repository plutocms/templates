The first hour of the day sets the pace for the rest of it. Mine is simple.

## Coffee first

I make one cup, by hand. It takes five minutes, and I do not look at a screen while it brews.

## A short walk

Ten minutes outside, at any weather. The walk is not exercise. It is time to think.

## One page of notes

I write one page: what I want to finish today, and one thing I learned yesterday. Then I start.
