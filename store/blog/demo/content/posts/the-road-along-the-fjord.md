The road is narrow, the speed limit is low, and you will want to stop every ten minutes. Plan for three days, not one.

## Day one

From the airport to the first ferry. The crossing takes twenty minutes, and the coffee on board is better than it looks.

## Day two

The longest part, with a climb to a viewpoint above the water. Go early: by noon the car park is full.

## Day three

The second ferry, and a slow drive to the town at the end of the fjord. We had fish soup by the harbour and did not talk much.
