The bus drops you at the first harbour. From there, the path follows the edge of the land, and the only rule is to keep the sea on your right.

## The path

It goes up and down all day. Some parts are wide and grassy, and some are a line of stones between the gorse and the drop.

## The lunch stop

Halfway, a bench looks over a bay with no road to it. I ate my sandwich there and counted eleven boats.

## The end

The second harbour has a café that closes at five. Plan for that. I did not.
