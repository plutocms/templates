A year ago I decided to publish one post every week. Here is what I learned.

## Short posts are fine

Most weeks, a post of 300 words was enough. A short post that is out is better than a long one in drafts.

## Keep a list of ideas

When an idea comes, it goes on the list. On writing day I pick one, and I never start from an empty page.

## Read it aloud

Before I publish, I read the post aloud. Each sentence that is hard to say gets shorter.

> Write the post you want to read.
