## Who we are

Low Tide Journal is written by Mara, Tomas and Iris. We walk more than we drive, cook in small kitchens, and write about the places in between.

::callout{type="tip" title="Write to us" text="Tell us about a walk, a market or a recipe from your corner of the map."}
::

## Why a journal

We write slowly, a few posts a month. Each one is a walk, a meal or a night outside, told the way it happened.
